I am currently completing my MSc in Physics—specializing in Complex Systems & Big Data—at the University of Rome Tor Vergata. My academic path is driven by a strong interest in quantitative data analysis, mathematical modeling, and computational physics.

Having transitioned from a classical humanities background to STEM, I have developed a strong flexibility of thought and a lateral approach to problem-solving. My core expertise lies in advanced statistical mechanics, optimization, and the modeling of complex systems, which I apply to analyze diverse phenomena, from financial networks to socio-physics. While this analytical and computational foundation is my primary focus, I also have practical experience with Machine Learning as a complementary tool. On the technical side, Python is my primary programming language, supported by a solid working knowledge of C. I am a native Italian speaker and fully fluent in English, which allows me to comfortably work and collaborate in international environments.

I highly value teamwork and collaborative environments. Within a group, I take a proactive approach to help coordinate tasks, sharing responsibilities to drive our projects forward. I tackle challenges with dedication, tenacity, and perseverance, always committing to deliver my work with absolute precision and punctuality.

Finally, I place great emphasis on how results are shared and communicated. I am highly proficient in LaTeX for drafting comprehensive and rigorous scientific reports, and I apply my personal passion for visual design to craft clear, high-impact PowerPoint presentations. I firmly believe that rigorous data analysis is most valuable when its insights are made accessible through accurate and well-crafted visual storytelling.
