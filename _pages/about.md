I am currently completing my MSc in Physics—specializing in Complex Systems & Big Data—at the University of Rome Tor Vergata. My academic and professional path is driven by quantitative data analysis, risk modeling, and predictive forecasting. I thrive on extracting order from chaos, translating massive, noisy datasets into clear, actionable mathematical models.

Having transitioned from a classical humanities background to STEM, I have developed a high flexibility of thought and a lateral approach to problem-solving. Through my academic research, I have built a robust expertise in applying advanced statistical mechanics, Information Theory, and optimization to real-world phenomena. From modeling structural phase shifts and extreme risks in complex datasets to filtering corporate diversification noise in macroscopic financial networks, my focus is always on understanding how systems behave and predicting their future states.

My analytical foundation is supported by a highly efficient technical and computational skill set. I primarily develop in Python—leveraging Pandas and NetworkX to handle large-scale datasets and extract structural insights—alongside a solid working knowledge of C for High-Performance Computing environments. Although Machine Learning is not my primary analytical focus, I have a deep theoretical understanding of its underlying mechanics and I am fully capable of practically implementing ML models to complement my statistical research. Additionally, I utilize Artificial Intelligence as a strategic support tool to streamline my workflow and accelerate the achievement of project goals. However, I approach these technologies with strict critical oversight: I systematically validate all AI-generated results, verify sources, and independently test algorithmic logic. This ensures I maximize operational efficiency while maintaining absolute control over the accuracy and scientific rigor of my final outputs.

I value stable, structured, and highly collaborative environments. Within a team, I take a proactive approach to coordinate tasks and share responsibilities to drive projects forward efficiently. I tackle challenges with dedication, tenacity, and perseverance, with a strict commitment to absolute precision, punctuality, and a healthy, sustainable approach to work. Fully fluent in English and native in Italian, I comfortably operate in international and multidisciplinary settings.

Finally, I place value on how results are communicated. I am highly proficient in LaTeX for drafting comprehensive scientific reports, but I also apply my personal passion for visual design to craft clear, high-impact PowerPoint presentations and clean data visualizations. I firmly believe that complex data analysis achieves its true purpose only when its insights are made accessible, elegant, and perfectly clear to decision-makers.
