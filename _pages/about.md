I am currently completing my MSc in Physics—specializing in Complex Systems & Big Data—at the University of Rome Tor Vergata. My academic and professional path is driven by quantitative data analysis, risk modeling, and predictive forecasting. Specifically, I am currently finalizing my thesis centered on network analysis applied to empirical economic networks, focusing on product-level value chains. I thrive on extracting order from chaos, translating massive, noisy datasets into clear, actionable mathematical models.   

Having transitioned from a classical humanities background to STEM, I have developed a high flexibility of thought and a lateral approach to problem-solving. This flexibility and adaptability are qualities I have also cultivated on a personal level, having moved from Calabria to Rome at just 16 years old. 

Through my academic research, I have built a robust expertise in applying advanced statistical mechanics, Information Theory, and optimization to real-world phenomena.   My analytical foundation is supported by a highly efficient technical and computational skill set. I primarily develop in Python—leveraging Pandas and NetworkX to handle large-scale datasets and extract structural insights—alongside a solid working knowledge of C for High-Performance Computing environments. Although Machine Learning is not my primary analytical focus, I have a deep theoretical understanding of its underlying mechanics and I am fully capable of practically implementing ML models. Additionally, I utilize Artificial Intelligence as a strategic support tool, while maintaining strict critical oversight to maximize operational efficiency without compromising scientific rigor.   

I value stable, structured, and highly collaborative environments. My approach to teamwork and leadership is rooted in my 5 years of scouting. Furthermore, my sense of responsibility, autonomy, and organization have been forged by the necessity of balancing university deadlines with my family duties, being the eldest sister of three siblings (the youngest of whom is 5 years old). I am fully fluent in English and native in Italian, which allows me to comfortably operate in international settings. Driven by a strong curiosity, in my free time I love traveling to engage with different cultures and practice intermediate-level trekking.   

Finally, I place great value on how results are communicated. I communicate effectively also thanks to my humanities background and my strong interest in culture (books, cinema, music, podcasts). I am highly proficient in LaTeX for drafting scientific reports, but I also apply my deep personal passion for art—ranging from painting to sculpture and photography—to curate the aesthetics and craft high-impact presentations and clean data visualizations. I firmly believe that complex data analysis achieves its true purpose only when its insights are made accessible, elegant, and perfectly clear to decision-makers.
