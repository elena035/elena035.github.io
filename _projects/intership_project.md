**Tools:** Python, Network Science, Pandas, Bipartite Projections
*Context: Master's Internship at the University of Rome "Tor Vergata"*

* Integrated firm-level supply chain data with RBICS revenue classifications to construct a granular, product-level global value chain.
* Conducted a temporal persistence analysis (2018-2023), proving the existence of a robust economic backbone despite the topological noise generated by corporate diversification.
* Established the methodological necessity of maximum-entropy null models (DBCM) to statistically filter spurious commercial dependencies.
