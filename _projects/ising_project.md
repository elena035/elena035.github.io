**Tools:** Python, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, MPI (Parallel Computing)
*Context: Computational Physics & High-Performance Computing Project*

* Developed a parallelized Monte Carlo simulation of the 2D Ising model to investigate phase transitions, thermal fluctuations, and spontaneous symmetry breaking.
* Conducted finite-size scaling analysis on multiple lattice dimensions (up to 240x240), successfully validating the numerical magnetization results against the exact analytical solution.
* Visualized microscopic spin configurations and dynamic equilibrium states across disordered, critical, and ordered phases through automated static snapshots and animated GIFs.
* Performed a rigorous strong scaling and speedup analysis, evaluating the impact of MPI halo exchange communication overhead and Amdahl's Law limits on a multicore architecture.
