**Tools:** Simulated Annealing, Metropolis Algorithm, Combinatorial Optimization, Statistical Mechanics
*Context: Academic Presentation (Optimization and Statistical Mechanics)*

* Addressed the Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP)—a classic combinatorial optimization challenge—by applying statistical mechanics tools and principles.
* Implemented an iterative Simulated Annealing algorithm using the Metropolis acceptance criterion to simulate thermal cooling, preventing the system from getting trapped in metastable states and favoring convergence to the ground state.
* **Result:** Validated the algorithm's stability on synthetic modular networks and successfully optimized the initial temperature ($T_{max}$) for a real-world macroscopic routing problem of 500 global cities. 
* Demonstrated that the execution time of this heuristic method scales significantly better than exact brute-force algorithms, making it highly advantageous for large-scale logistical applications.
